What is the Nipah Virus?

Nipah Virus (NiV) is a zoonotic virus transmitted from animals to humans. Yes, an animal-to-human virus is possible. Many of us do not know this, but animals and humans share some traits. Therefore, within these traits, a virus between an animal and a human can happen anytime soon. Two animals that can cause a Nipah Virus are pigs and Fruit bats. This year, in 2024, a Nipah virus broke out in India. A case involved a 14-year-old boy who got sick and died after eating hog plum fruit from his neighborhood [2]. How is this possible? How can hog plum fruit be poisonous?
