ILOVEYOU

The seven letter word stands for I Love You. But what exactly was ILOVEYOU. The ILOVEYOU was a virus that spread through email attachments with the subject line "ILOVEYOU" and anybody who clicked on it got a virus. Pinoy hacker Onel de Guzman was the one who created the virus. But why did the guy actually create this virus? Was he in love with someone?
